How I got in2 this

It started by accident. Between cities — New York, DC, Los Angeles, Boston, Italy, Miami — I was working remotely and needed a way to live somewhere for a month at a time without paying rent in four time zones.

Pet sitting turned out to be the answer. Someone trusts you with their home and, more importantly, with their dog or cat. You keep the routines, send the photo updates, learn each pet's quirks, and hand everything back exactly how you found it. Except the pet is usually a little more spoiled.

What surprised me is how much I loved it. As a runner, I already live on routine and early mornings, which happens to be exactly what dogs want from a human. Walks happen on time. Food happens on time. Zoomies happen whenever they want, and that is fine too.

Now that I am back home in Cyprus, I kept it going as my favorite side project. Fewer flights, same photo updates.
